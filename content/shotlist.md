# Freepik shot list: GazMin Commodities

For whoever downloads from the client's Freepik account (now branded Magnific). Download by hand: Freepik's terms (section 2) forbid scripts and bots, and the account can be cancelled for it.

## Where the files go

- Photos: `assets/freepik/`, named exactly as in the **File** column (`.jpg`).
- Licences: `assets/freepik/licences/`, one PDF per photo, same name (`bv-coffee.pdf`). Freepik offers the licence PDF in the download history.
- Tell the developer when a batch lands. Nothing else is needed: the build converts, crops and grades the photos.

## Every photo

- **Real photography only.** In Freepik's search filters, exclude AI-generated content. Most of the current site's images look AI-made, and that is a large part of why it reads as cheap.
- **Low-key or golden-hour light, warm highlights.** The site is night navy and gold, so dark, rich frames fit; flat midday light and white studio backgrounds do not.
- **No faces looking into the camera, no posed smiles, no visible text, logos or watermarks.**
- **Size:** at least 2400px wide for the page heroes, at least 1600px wide for everything else. Landscape unless the Crop column says otherwise.
- One subject per frame, cropped tight. A close-up of the commodity itself beats a generic farm or office.
